## Story Collection Protocol

### Before Collecting

1. **Explain Purpose** - What will the story be used for?
2. **Consent Options** - Internal only, external-lite, or full external?
3. **Identity Protection** - Name, anonymized, or pseudonym?
4. **Review Rights** - Storyteller can review before publishing

### During Collection

1. **Listen First** - Don't rush to questions
2. **Ask About Their Journey** - How did they come to this?
3. **Capture Emotion** - What surprised/moved/frustrated them?
4. **Record Details** - Place, time, who was there
5. **Note Quotes** - Direct voice is powerful

### After Collection

1. **Transcribe** - Get words written down
2. **Review with Storyteller** - Did we capture it right?
3. **Log Consent** - Document in Empathy Ledger
4. **Create Entry** - Add to EL with proper metadata
5. **Link to Project** - Connect story to project
