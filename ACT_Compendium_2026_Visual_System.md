# ACT Compendium 2026 Visual System (Draft)

Purpose: make the compendium legible, grounded, and consent-safe while keeping LCAA and ALMA visible.

## Principles

- Land and community first; visuals must feel place-led.
- Consent before visuals; no images or stories without clear scope.
- System-level focus; avoid individual profiling or ranking.
- Clarity over spectacle; simple, low-noise diagrams.
- Conservation-first language and imagery.

## Core diagram set

V1. ACT Core Model map
- Intent: show land practice + enterprise + LCAA + ALMA in one view.
- Inputs: ACT Core Model, compendium sections 1 to 6.
- Output: single page map (A4 landscape + 16:9).

V2. LCAA loop with 2026 examples
- Intent: show the method in motion, not as a slogan.
- Inputs: 2 to 4 real examples from compendium focus areas.
- Output: circular diagram with short callouts.

V3. ALMA reporting loop
- Intent: show story -> signal -> shift -> scope.
- Inputs: 2 vignette examples, ALMA signal families.
- Output: loop diagram + short caption.

V4. Enterprise pathway loops
- Intent: show Goods and Harvest as local capability loops.
- Inputs: Goods manufacturing flow, Harvest operations flow.
- Output: two loop diagrams with shared legend.

V5. Ecosystem map
- Intent: show outputs + expressions + governance links.
- Inputs: Section 5 ecosystem map.
- Output: node map with three layers.

V6. Consent and data flow map
- Intent: show Empathy Ledger as source of truth and consent gate.
- Inputs: Section 11 to 14 data flow.
- Output: flow diagram with consent gates.

V7. Project snapshot grid (optional)
- Intent: quick scan of active projects with 1 line each.
- Inputs: active project list.
- Output: 3 column grid.

V8. Impact learning timeline (optional)
- Intent: show how learning moves through the year.
- Inputs: roadmap sections.
- Output: timeline with ALMA checkpoints.

## Section placement

- Sections 1 to 4: V1 or V2 + a land practice hero image.
- Sections 7 to 8: V5 + 6 to 10 project photos.
- Sections 11 to 14: V3 + story vignette panel.
- Sections 15 to 18: V4 + V6 (and V8 if needed).

## Media selection rules

- Use Empathy Ledger as the source of truth.
- Confirm consent scope before placement (internal vs external).
- Avoid identifiable faces unless explicit consent allows it.
- Do not include addresses or personal identifiers in captions.
- Prioritize community-led imagery over stock.

## Video and motion

Video should carry lived experience and practical learning, not marketing gloss.

Recommended cuts:

- 30 to 60 seconds: internal snapshot and context setting.
- 2 to 4 minutes: project vignette with clear takeaway.
- 6 to 10 minutes: deep practice and process (internal only).

Tagging requirements (Empathy Ledger):

- compendium-2026
- project:<slug>
- scope:internal | scope:external-lite | scope:external
- alma:<signal>

Notes:

- Always include a short summary and transcript.
- Use consented stills for thumbnails.

## Tools and workflow

- Diagrams: Figma or Illustrator.
- Maps: QGIS or simple vector maps (no individual-level data).
- Charts: simple bar or timeline graphics only when necessary.
- AI support: draft exploration only; final visuals require human and community review.
