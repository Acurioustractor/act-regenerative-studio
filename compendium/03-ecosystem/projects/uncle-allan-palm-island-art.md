# Uncle Allan Palm Island Art

**Art as cultural transmission - Elder practice that keeps knowledge alive.**

Uncle Allan Palm Island Art documents and supports the art practice of Uncle Allan, a Palm Island Elder whose work carries cultural knowledge, community connection, and intergenerational wisdom.

---

## Purpose

Art isn't just expression - it's transmission. Uncle Allan's practice:

- **Carries knowledge** - Cultural stories embedded in visual form
- **Connects generations** - Young people learning through creating
- **Preserves tradition** - Techniques and symbols that might otherwise be lost
- **Builds connection** - Art that brings community together
