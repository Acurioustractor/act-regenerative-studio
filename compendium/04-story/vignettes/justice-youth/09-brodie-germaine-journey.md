# Brodie Germaine's Journey: From Housing Commission to Advocate

> "I didn't come from a place where people expected you to succeed. But someone believed in me when I couldn't believe in myself. Now I'm that person for others."

---

## Story Summary

Brodie Germaine's story is one of transformation—from a young person navigating the challenges of housing commission life to a powerful advocate working to change the systems that nearly failed him.

Growing up, Brodie experienced firsthand what it means to fall through the cracks. The justice system, the education system, the welfare system—each one presented barriers that could have defined his future. Instead, with the support of people who saw his potential rather than his circumstances, Brodie found pathways forward.

Today, Brodie works at the intersection of youth justice and community development. His lived experience isn't just background—it's expertise. When young people in similar circumstances see Brodie, they see possibility. When system designers hear Brodie speak, they understand impact in ways that statistics never convey.

Brodie's journey illustrates a core ACT belief: those closest to the problem are closest to the solution. His advocacy isn't about speaking for young people—it's about creating space for their voices to be heard directly.

The transformation from "service recipient" to "system changer" didn't happen overnight. It required patient investment from organizations willing to let Brodie grow at his own pace, make mistakes, and develop his voice. That patience paid off in ways no one could have predicted.
