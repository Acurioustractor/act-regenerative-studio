# ACT 2026 Snapshot (Shareable Draft)

Internal/external-lite summary. Partner-specific nuance stays internal.

## One-liner
A regenerative innovation studio stewarding a working farm on Jinibara Country.

## What we are building
ACT grows co-stewardship and shared governance through land, story, and action. The farm is held as a commons, and the studio is a toolkit communities can hold, shape, and eventually outgrow.

## 2026 focus
- Innovation studio and products: tools designed for community ownership, clarity, and handover.
- Enterprise pathways: The Harvest as a grounded Witta enterprise hub, and Goods on Country moving to reliable local manufacturing.
- Land stewardship: conservation-first practice, land capacity respected, co-stewardship readiness built through relationship and protocol.

## Core model
- LCAA (Listen, Curiosity, Action, Art) is our operating rhythm.
- ALMA (Adaptive Learning for Meaningful Accountability) is our impact and learning model.
- Empathy Ledger is the core impact tool for consented stories and evidence.

## Core outputs and expressions
Outputs (infrastructure):
Empathy Ledger, JusticeHub, Goods on Country, Black Cockatoo Valley, The Harvest, ACT Farm, Art practice.

**Expressions (how people experience the work):**
Residencies, workshops, events, gatherings, seasonal moments, and practical making roles led by lived experience.

## Why it matters
We build community-owned infrastructure that protects dignity, health, and cultural authority while creating real enterprise pathways and shared learning.

## Consent and governance
Consent is explicit, ongoing, and revocable. 
Cultural sovereignty leads decision-making. 
Sacred knowledge is protected by hard blocks. 
We observe systems, never optimise people.

## Signals we watch in 2026
- Land health and capacity remain central in planning and delivery.
- Community authority is visible in decisions and ownership pathways.
- Enterprise pathways (Harvest, Goods) are stable and locally led.
- Consent integrity is protected in every story and dataset.
- Learning flows back into design through ALMA and Empathy Ledger.

## A Curious Tractor
We design tools for handover, not dependency. 
Beautiful Obsolescence means our success is measured by how well communities can take ownership and move beyond us.
