# Goods on Country

**"Your waste, your wealth"**

Circular-economy venture co-designing essential products (beds, mattresses, washing machines) for remote communities while converting local waste into manufacturing inputs.

---

## The Problem

Remote communities face dual extraction:
- Pay premium prices for essential goods
- Products often culturally inappropriate, low quality, or unavailable
- Plastic waste accumulates with no local recycling
- Value extracted through expensive products AND waste they can't process

**Health impacts:**
- Unsafe/dirty mattresses affecting sleep and hygiene
- Limited access to essential appliances
- Dignity compromised by poor-quality goods

---

## Our Approach

Community-owned manufacturing that transforms plastic waste into products communities actually need:
- Co-designed with communities
- Manufactured using local "waste"
- Sold/distributed through community-controlled structures
- 40% of profits flow back to source communities

## How It Works

```
1. CO-DESIGN
   Communities identify needed products
   and design specifications
          ↓
2. WASTE COLLECTION
   Plastic waste gathered from communities
   (becomes input material)
          ↓
3. MANUFACTURING
   Products made from recycled plastic
   + other inputs
          ↓
4. DISTRIBUTION
   Sold back to communities at fair prices
   (or gifted depending on model)
          ↓
5. PROFIT-SHARING
   40% of profits to source communities
          ↓
6. CAPACITY TRANSFER
   Communities learn manufacturing
   Eventually operate independently
```
