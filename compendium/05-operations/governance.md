# Governance & Consent

Community authority carries the highest weight, even when it complicates delivery.

---

## Governance Structure

### Current State
- Founder-led with community advisory
- Traditional Owner consultation on land-based work
- Transparency in financial decisions

### Future Goal
- Indigenous-majority board on place-based projects
- Community co-stewardship with shared governance
- Beautiful obsolescence: ACT steps back as communities take ownership

## Governance Principles

### 1. Community Voice in All Major Decisions
- Traditional Owner consultation on land-based work
- Stakeholder representation across communities served
- Elder wisdom integrated into strategic planning

### 2. Transparency by Default
- Financial decisions documented and accessible
- Decision rationale recorded
- Open to community scrutiny

### 3. Authority Flows to Community
- Governance structures shift power to communities
- Exit strategies built into every project
- Community veto power on major decisions affecting them

## Governance Handover Pathway

| Phase | Timeline | Governance |
|-------|----------|------------|
| 1 | Year 1-2 | ACT-led with community advisory |
| 2 | Year 3-5 | Co-governance (shared decision-making) |
| 3 | Year 5+ | Community-majority board |
| 4 | Exit | Full community ownership and governance |

### Support During Transition
- Capacity building (financial management, governance)
- Ongoing technical support (as requested)
- Fundraising and partnership connections
- Replication toolkits
- Peer network with other communities

## Decision-Making Process

### Major Decisions
```
1. Identify decision scope and affected communities
2. Community consultation (appropriate format)
3. Document options and trade-offs
4. Community input on preferred approach
5. Decision with documented rationale
6. Implementation with feedback loops
7. Review and course-correct as needed
```

### When Community Says No
- Respect the decision
- Don't push for reconsideration
- Document the reasoning
- Adjust plans accordingly
- Return only if community initiates

---

## Partnership Principles

### Long-Term Relationships Over Transactional Projects
- Multi-year commitments, not one-off interventions
- Trust-building before agenda-setting
- Presence on Country and in communities
- Reciprocal learning, not extraction
- Exit pathways planned from the start

### Community-Led Design
- Communities set priorities, not ACT
- Co-design workshops, not consultations
- Iterate based on community feedback
- Respect when communities say "no"
- Flexible to community timing and rhythms

### Power-Sharing
- Community governance from day one
- Transparent decision-making
- Shared ownership structures (40% profit)
- Community veto power on major decisions
- Sunset clauses and handover plans

---

## 40% Profit-Sharing

### How It Works
- Legal structures allocate 40% of profits to source communities
- Community decides how funds are allocated
- Transparent accounting and reporting
- Tracked from day one, even before profitability
- No profit extraction by ACT founders

### Applied To
- Goods on Country manufacturing revenue
- Black Cockatoo Valley eco-cottage income
- Art sales and licensing
- Consulting and training fees
- Any commercial activity

## Consent in Governance

### Consent for Data and Stories
- OCAP principles enforced (see [Cultural Protocols](cultural-protocols.md))
- Elder review for cultural content
- Community authority over sharing decisions

### Consent for Partnership
- Clear agreements before work begins
- Exit clauses in all partnerships
- Community can end partnership at any time
- Data remains with community on exit

### Consent for Representation
- Community approves how they're represented
- No use of community name without permission
- No claims without community validation
- Attribution and recognition protocols

## Accountability Mechanisms

### To Communities
- Quarterly impact reviews (open meetings)
- Annual celebration and reflection
- Real-time dashboards (community access)
- Community decides what's shared externally

### To Funders
- Grant reports with community voice
- Honest assessment including challenges
- ROI and efficiency metrics
- Learning documentation

### To Movement
- Annual public impact report
- Blog posts and case studies
- Sharing failures and learnings
- Open-source tools and methods
