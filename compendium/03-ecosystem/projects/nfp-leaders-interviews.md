# NFP leaders interview project

**Capturing sector wisdom through conversations with leaders.**

The NFP Leaders Interview Project is a research initiative capturing insights from leaders across Australia's not-for-profit sector - documenting what works, what doesn't, and what the sector needs to evolve.

---

## Purpose

The NFP sector holds enormous knowledge about community, systems change, and social impact - but much of it stays locked in individuals' heads. This project:

- **Captures wisdom** - Recording experienced leaders' insights
- **Maps patterns** - Finding common themes across interviews
- **Shares learning** - Making insights available to the sector
- **Preserves knowledge** - Documenting before leaders retire or move on
