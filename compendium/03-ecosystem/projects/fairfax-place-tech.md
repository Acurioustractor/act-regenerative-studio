# Fairfax & PLACE tech

**Exploring how technology can serve place-based communities.**

Fairfax & PLACE tech is a research partnership exploring how technology can be designed to serve place-based communities - not extract from them.

---

## Purpose

Most technology is designed for anywhere and everywhere, missing what makes each place unique. This partnership explores:

- **Place-first design** - Technology that honours local context
- **Community benefit** - Tech that serves rather than extracts
- **Practical applications** - Tools that address real local needs
- **Research sharing** - Publishing what we learn
