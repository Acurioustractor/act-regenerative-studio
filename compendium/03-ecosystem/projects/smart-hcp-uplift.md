# SMART HCP GP Uplift Project

**Improving GP pathways to recovery support.**

The SMART HCP GP Uplift Project works to improve how general practitioners connect patients to recovery support services, particularly SMART Recovery programs.

---

## Purpose

GPs are often the first point of contact for people struggling with addiction, but referral pathways to peer support like SMART Recovery are unclear or unknown. This project:

- **Trains GPs** - Understanding recovery pathways
- **Builds pathways** - Clear referral processes
- **Measures outcomes** - Tracking what works
- **Improves connection** - Primary care to peer support
