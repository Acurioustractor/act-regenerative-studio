# Palm Island Community Company (PICC)

**Community-controlled development led by Palm Island people**

Palm Island Community Company is an Aboriginal community-controlled organization serving the Palm Island community. ACT's role is to support PICC's vision with technical capacity, not to direct it. Everything happens at PICC's pace, on PICC's terms.

---

## Purpose

Palm Island has a complex history with outside organizations. Too often, well-meaning groups have arrived with plans developed elsewhere, implemented programs without genuine consultation, then left—taking knowledge and resources with them.

PICC represents a different model. Community members control the organization. Decisions are made locally. External partners like ACT provide support, but authority stays with the community.

The partnership with ACT focuses on specific areas where technical capacity adds value:
- Story sovereignty and digital infrastructure
- Capacity building in multimedia and documentation
- Technical support for community-led initiatives
- Connection to broader networks while maintaining local control

What doesn't happen: ACT doesn't tell PICC what to do. ACT doesn't take stories off the island without consent. ACT doesn't promise outcomes it can't deliver.
