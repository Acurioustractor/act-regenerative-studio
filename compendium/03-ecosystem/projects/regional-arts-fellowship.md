# Regional Arts Fellowship

**Supporting artists who work with and for regional communities.**

The Regional Arts Fellowship supports artists whose practice is rooted in regional and rural communities. It recognises that some of Australia's most important creative work happens far from the cities.

---

## Purpose

Regional arts practice faces unique challenges - distance, funding models designed for cities, lack of infrastructure. This fellowship provides:

- **Financial support** - Resources to sustain practice
- **Network connection** - Links to other regional practitioners
- **Documentation** - Capturing and sharing regional arts approaches
- **Visibility** - Amplifying regional work nationally
