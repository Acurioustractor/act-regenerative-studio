# School Partnership Success Stories

> "Our attendance rates improved by 20% for students engaged with Oonchiumpa. That's not a coincidence—that's wraparound support actually working."

---

## Story Summary

Schools see the symptoms of what's happening in young people's lives. Disengagement, behavior issues, declining attendance—teachers know something is wrong, but school systems often can't address the underlying causes.

The Oonchiumpa school partnerships bridge this gap. When schools identify students at risk, they can connect them with support that extends beyond the classroom. Oonchiumpa workers don't just visit schools—they become part of the ecosystem, understanding each student's context and coordinating support across multiple domains.

The partnership model evolved from initial skepticism to active collaboration:

**Phase 1 - Testing**: Schools cautiously referred students they'd "tried everything" with. Oonchiumpa took on these hard cases, knowing early wins would build trust.

**Phase 2 - Building**: Early successes led to more referrals. Schools began inviting Oonchiumpa into planning meetings, recognizing that external perspective added value.

**Phase 3 - Integration**: Mature partnerships feature regular communication, shared goals, and coordinated responses to crises. Oonchiumpa isn't an external service—it's part of how the school supports students.

Teacher feedback highlights practical changes:
- "I have someone to call when a student is spiraling. Before, I'd just watch it happen."
- "They understand what's going on at home. I can focus on teaching."
- "Students know Oonchiumpa is there for them even outside school hours."

The attendance data tells part of the story—20% improvement for engaged students is significant. But numbers don't capture the individual transformations: students who were counting down to dropping out now planning for graduation, behavior issues resolving as underlying stressors are addressed, teachers feeling supported instead of overwhelmed.
