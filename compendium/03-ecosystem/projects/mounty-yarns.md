# Mounty Yarns

**Storytelling that connects youth to justice reform in Melbourne.**

Mounty Yarns is a Melbourne-based project connecting storytelling with youth justice. Through yarning circles, creative expression, and community connection, young people find voice and communities find evidence for change.

---

## Purpose

Melbourne's youth justice challenges mirror the national pattern - Indigenous overrepresentation, system responses that harm more than help, communities calling for alternatives. Mounty Yarns responds by:

- **Yarning circles** - Creating space for story and connection
- **Youth voice** - Amplifying what young people say they need
- **Community evidence** - Building the case for justice reinvestment
- **Creative expression** - Art as pathway to healing and advocacy
