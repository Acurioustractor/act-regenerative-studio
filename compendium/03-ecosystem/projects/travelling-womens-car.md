# Travelling women's car

**A mobile space for women's stories and cultural preservation.**

The Travelling Women's Car is a mobile storytelling and cultural preservation project - a vehicle that travels to communities, creating space for women to share stories and preserve cultural knowledge.

---

## Purpose

Some stories need to travel to where people are. The Travelling Women's Car:

- **Goes to community** - Not waiting for people to come to us
- **Women's space** - Safe environment for women's stories
- **Cultural preservation** - Recording knowledge that might otherwise be lost
- **Connection** - Linking women across geography
