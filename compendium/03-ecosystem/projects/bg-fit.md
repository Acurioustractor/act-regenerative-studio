# BG Fit

**Lived-experience leadership transforming youth justice in Mount Isa.**

BG Fit is a youth wellbeing and justice program in Mount Isa, Queensland, led by people with lived experience of the systems young people face. The program demonstrates that those who've walked the path are best positioned to guide others through it.

---

## Purpose

Mount Isa faces significant challenges around youth justice, with Indigenous young people dramatically overrepresented in detention. BG Fit responds by:

- **Lived experience leadership** - Program led by people who understand the journey
- **Place-based response** - Designed for Mount Isa's specific context
- **Wellbeing focus** - Addressing root causes, not just symptoms
- **Cultural connection** - Grounded in local Indigenous knowledge and practice
