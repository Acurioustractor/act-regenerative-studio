# Risks & Dependencies

Naming risk early is part of stewardship. We do not hide it in the margins.

> "The risk we ignore is the one that shows up in the work."

---

## Risk Categories

### 1. Capacity Risk
**Small team carrying multi-project delivery**

| Factor | Impact | Mitigation |
|--------|--------|------------|
| Team stretch | Burnout, quality drops | Fewer priorities, clearer scope |
| Key person dependency | Single points of failure | Documentation, skill sharing |
| Volunteer burnout | Community disengagement | Sustainable ask, recognition |
| Seasonal pressure | Concentration of work | Better rhythms, flexibility |

**Signals to watch:**
- Response times increasing
- Documentation falling behind
- Team morale indicators
- Volunteer retention rates

### 2. Consent Risk
**Misuse of stories or data without proper review**

| Factor | Impact | Mitigation |
|--------|--------|------------|
| Story misuse | Trust breach, harm | Elder review, consent gates |
| Data creep | Privacy violation | OCAP enforcement, audits |
| AI drift | Extractive outputs | ALMA guardrails, human review |
| Media misuse | Cultural harm | Provenance tracking, consent flags |

**Signals to watch:**
- Consent review backlog
- Stories used without refresh
- AI outputs needing correction
- Community concerns raised

### 3. Enterprise Risk
**Supply chain and operational reliability**

| Factor | Impact | Mitigation |
|--------|--------|------------|
| Supply disruption | Production halts | Multiple suppliers, inventory |
| Quality issues | Reputation damage | QA processes, feedback loops |
| Cash flow gaps | Operations at risk | Revenue diversification |
| Partner dependency | Single-point failure | Relationship diversification |

**Signals to watch:**
- Production consistency metrics
- Customer satisfaction feedback
- Revenue pipeline health
- Partner relationship quality

### 4. Land Risk
**Overextension beyond land or community readiness**

| Factor | Impact | Mitigation |
|--------|--------|------------|
| Overuse | Habitat degradation | Capacity limits, monitoring |
| Relationship strain | Trust erosion | Deeper listening, slower pace |
| Governance gaps | Decision delays | Clearer pathways, protocols |
| Climate impacts | Operational disruption | Adaptation planning |

**Signals to watch:**
- Land health indicators
- Traditional Owner engagement quality
- Governance pathway progress
- Weather and climate patterns

### 5. AI Risk
**Misalignment or extractive outputs**

| Factor | Impact | Mitigation |
|--------|--------|------------|
| Output drift | Off-brand, off-values | ALMA guardrails, review |
| Data leakage | Privacy breach | Local-first, consent gates |
| Dependence | Skill atrophy | Human-first design |
| Hallucination | Misinformation | Verification workflows |

**Signals to watch:**
- AI output quality metrics
- Human override frequency
- Community feedback on AI interactions
- Skills development progress

## Risk Mitigation Principles

### Discipline Over Speed
Mitigation is discipline: fewer priorities, deeper listening, and clear guardrails.

### Early Warning Systems
- Weekly team check-ins include risk review
- Monthly community feedback synthesis
- Quarterly risk assessment updates
- Annual risk audit and reset

### Response Protocols

**When risk materializes:**
1. **Acknowledge** - Name it clearly, don't minimize
2. **Contain** - Limit immediate harm
3. **Communicate** - Tell affected parties first
4. **Learn** - Update processes, share lessons
5. **Restore** - Repair relationships, rebuild trust

### Escalation Matrix

| Severity | Response Time | Who Decides | Who's Informed |
|----------|---------------|-------------|----------------|
| Critical | Immediate | Leadership | All stakeholders |
| High | 24 hours | Project lead | Leadership, affected parties |
| Medium | 1 week | Team | Project lead |
| Low | Monthly review | Team | Documented |
