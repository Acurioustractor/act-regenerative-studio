# ANAT SPECTRA 2025

**Exploring the intersection of art, technology, and community storytelling.**

ANAT SPECTRA 2025 is a collaboration with the Australian Network for Art and Technology (ANAT), exploring how creative practice and emerging technology can serve community storytelling.

---

## Purpose

ANAT's SPECTRA program supports artists working at the intersection of art, science, and technology. This collaboration explores:

- **Technology for community voice** - How can emerging tech serve storytelling?
- **Art as evidence** - Creative practice that builds the case for change
- **Ethical technology** - Building tools that respect rather than extract
- **Cross-disciplinary learning** - Artists, technologists, and communities learning together
