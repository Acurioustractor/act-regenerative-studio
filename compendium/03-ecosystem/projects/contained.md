# Contained

**An experiential installation that makes you feel what detention does.**

Contained is an art installation that invites participants into an embodied experience of what it feels like to be contained - detained, restricted, controlled. It's not a simulation of prison; it's an invitation to feel, and through feeling, to question.

---

## Purpose

Statistics don't change hearts. Reports don't shift systems. But felt experience can.

Contained uses:
- **Spatial design** - Architecture that constrains and controls
- **Sensory immersion** - Sound, light, and space that evoke confinement
- **Guided reflection** - Prompts that connect feeling to understanding
- **Community voice** - Stories from people who've lived this reality

The installation asks: What does containment do to a person? What are we actually choosing when we choose detention?
