# SMART Connect

**Connecting people in recovery to community and each other.**

SMART Connect is a partnership with SMART Recovery to create connection pathways for people in recovery. Recovery works best when it's not done alone - this project ensures no one has to be.

---

## Purpose

Recovery from addiction is hard enough without isolation. SMART Connect provides:

- **Peer connection** - Meeting others on the same journey
- **Community integration** - Pathways to belonging beyond recovery groups
- **Story sharing** - Capturing and sharing recovery narratives
- **Support continuity** - Connection that doesn't end when meetings do
