## Deep Understanding Captured

### ACT's Identity & Mission
- Regenerative innovation ecosystem (not just "studio")
- Partnering with marginalised communities (especially First Nations)
- Dismantling extractive systems
- PTO (power take-off) metaphor - transferring capacity to community
- Designed for obsolescence - "handing over the keys"

### Legal & Financial Structure
- Dual-entity model: CLG (charity) + mission-locked trading company
- 40% profit-sharing to communities
- Multiple funding streams (grants, government contracts, social enterprise, impact investment)
- Mission-locked governance

### Core Methodology (LCAA)
- **Listen**: Deep listening to place, people, history, ancestral teachers
- **Curiosity**: Ask better questions, prototype, lean into unknown
- **Action**: Build tangible outputs through innovation, story, art
- **Art**: First form of revolution, challenge status quo

### Values System
- **Radical Humility**: No saviors, cultivating together
- **Decentralised Power**: Communities lead, sunset clauses, forkable IP
- **Creativity as Disruption**: Revolution starts with imagination
- **Truth-telling**: Name extractive systems, work to dismantle

### Land Practice (Black Cockatoo Valley)
- 150 acres (117 ha) on Jinibara lands
- Threatened species habitat (conservation first)
- Low-impact, limited-volume approach
- No extractive tourism
- Premium residencies fund restoration
- Community co-stewardship goal

### Voice & Tone Mastery
The skill now captures ACT's unique voice:
- Use farm metaphors naturally (seeds, harvest, cultivating)
- Balance practical wisdom with ambitious vision
- Warm invitation paired with systemic truth-telling
- Poetic language that illuminates rather than obscures
- Place-first, community-first framing
- Avoid overclaiming, luxury language, corporate speak

## How to Use This Skill

### For Content Work
When writing ANY ACT content (web pages, marketing, grants):
1. Invoke or reference this skill at the start
2. Identify which project/scope (Hub, BCV, JusticeHub, etc.)
3. Load appropriate references (always brand-core, conditionally others)
4. Apply voice guardrails
5. Deliver with appropriate CTAs and cross-links

### For Design Work
When designing ACT visuals or UI:
1. Reference Visual Language section in brand-core.md
2. Use earthy palette (Stone + Emerald)
3. Apply farm imagery and motifs
4. Maintain conservation-first framing
5. Keep layouts spacious and quiet

### For Strategy Work
When planning initiatives or communications:
1. Ground in LCAA methodology
2. Ensure community ownership is central
3. Include profit-sharing commitment where relevant
4. Frame in farm metaphor system
5. Design for obsolescence
