# JusticeHub

**"Justice by the community, for the community"**

Open-source justice network where grassroots programs can fork proven models, access AI-generated insights, and co-create culturally aligned governance.

---

## The Problem

Youth justice systems are failing globally:
- 58% recidivism
- Billions spent on detention that traumatizes instead of heals
- Top-down, punishment-focused approaches don't work
- Community-led alternatives exist but are fragmented, underfunded, hidden
- No infrastructure for sharing, adapting, scaling what works

---

## Our Approach

Open-source clearinghouse + AI-powered insights + community court framework:
- Programs can be "forked" (like software) and adapted to local context
- Communities design their own justice systems with evidence-based support
- Lived-experience leadership at the center

## Key Features

### For Youth
- Opportunity matching (apprenticeships, jobs, programs)
- Mentor connections (interests, location, cultural background)
- Living Libraries (youth stories valued as evidence)
- Skill-building pathways
- Peer support networks

### For Communities
- Program registry (forkable models with open-source tools)
- Community court templates
- AI insights (what works where, for whom)
- Impact tracking and storytelling
- Funding and partnership connections

### For System
- Evidence base of effective interventions
- Cross-community learning
- Policy advocacy tools
- Cost-benefit analysis
- Alternative sentencing frameworks

## LCAA Application

**Listen:**
- Youth shared being treated as problems, not people
- Communities had solutions but no platform to share
- Existing systems punitive, not restorative
- Cultural disconnect in mainstream justice

**Curiosity:**
- What if communities could fork justice models like code?
- How might AI reveal patterns across successful programs?
- Can youth stories become valid form of evidence?
- What would community courts look like?

**Action:**
- Built open-source platform for program sharing
- Developed AI matching for youth opportunities
- Created templates for community governance
- Integrated storytelling as methodology
- Made entire system forkable

**Art:**
- Living Libraries (youth as knowledge-holders)
- Story-driven campaigns (CONTAINED)
- Imaginative alternatives to detention
- Cultural celebration integrated

## Ethical Guardrails

JusticeHub must never:
- Profile individuals
- Create risk scores for youth
- Replace community judgment with algorithms
- Share data without consent
- Privilege efficiency over dignity

JusticeHub must always:
- Center lived-experience voice
- Respect cultural sovereignty
- Support community governance
- Make systems forkable and independent
- Design for handover
