# MMEIC - Justice Projects

**Elder-led justice reinvestment on Minjerribah (North Stradbroke Island).**

MMEIC Justice Projects is a partnership with the Minjerribah Moorgumpin Elders-In-Council, supporting their vision for community-led justice responses. ACT provides technical support; Elders hold authority.

---

## Purpose

Minjerribah (North Stradbroke Island) faces the same justice challenges as many Indigenous communities - overrepresentation in detention, under-investment in community solutions. MMEIC's response:

- **Elder authority** - Decisions made by those who hold cultural responsibility
- **Justice reinvestment** - Redirecting resources to community solutions
- **Storytelling** - Using narrative to build the evidence base
- **Youth pathways** - Creating alternatives to the justice system
