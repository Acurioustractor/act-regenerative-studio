# ALMA Model

**Adaptive Learning for Meaningful Accountability**

ALMA is our shared impact-and-learning model. It helps us notice system-level patterns without profiling people and keeps community authority central. ALMA is a way of paying attention, not scoring.

---

## Core Principle

> "Evidence is story, not surveillance. We do not profile people. We track system-level signals, learn from consented stories, and change practice in response."

---

## How ALMA Works

```
Community Stories (Empathy Ledger)
         ↓
    Consent Controls
         ↓
    ALMA Signal Reading
    (system-level, not individual)
         ↓
    Pattern Recognition
         ↓
    Learning Feedback
         ↓
    LCAA Practice Changes
         ↓
    Product & Enterprise Decisions
```

---

## ALMA Signals

Six lenses for reading stories at a system level:

| Signal | Question | Scale |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **Authority** | Who holds decision-making power? | Community / Practitioner / Partner |
| **Evidence** | What type of evidence is this? | Narrative / Outcome / Service demand |
| **Harm Risk** | What's the risk of sharing? | Low / Medium / High |
| **Capability** | How ready is the system to act? | Low / Medium / High |
| **Option Value** | Does this open future possibilities? | Low / Medium / High |
| **Community Value Return** | Does value flow back to community? | Low / Medium / High |

---

## ALMA Data Anchors

For each story or learning:

| Field | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| Story record | Empathy Ledger ID |
| Consent scope | INTERNAL ONLY / EXTERNAL-LITE / EXTERNAL |
| Cultural sensitivity | Low / Medium / High |
| Project slugs | Which projects this connects to |
| Place/Country | Geographic and cultural context |
| ALMA signal tags | The six lenses above |
| LCAA stage | Listen / Curiosity / Action / Art |

---

## ALMA in Reporting

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Story** | A consented narrative or quote from Empathy Ledger |
| **Signal** | ALMA lens notes at system level |
| **Shift** | What we changed or will change in practice (LCAA step) |
| **Scope** | What is shareable now vs internal-only |

### Example ALMA Report Entry

```
Story: "With Oonchiumpa support, M and her mother secured
       stable housing and M gained her own income."

Signal:
- Authority: lived experience
- Evidence: outcome
- Harm risk: medium (privacy)
- Capability: medium
- Option value: high
- Community value return: high

Shift: Action -> Art (support becomes pathway)

Scope: EXTERNAL-LITE (identity protected)
```

## What ALMA Is Not

| ALMA is NOT | ALMA IS |
|-------------|---------|
| Individual profiling | System-level pattern recognition |
| Risk scoring | Context-aware learning |
| Surveillance | Consent-based evidence |
| Extraction | Community-owned insight |
| Optimization of people | Observation of systems |

---

## Integration with Empathy Ledger

Empathy Ledger is the core impact tool. It holds consented stories, community voice, and evidence that feeds learning across projects.

**Flow:**
1. Stories created in Empathy Ledger with consent controls
2. Stories tagged with ALMA signals and project slugs
3. ALMA reads patterns at system level
4. Learning informs LCAA practice and product decisions
5. Community retains ownership and control

## ALMA Guardrails

### Non-Negotiables
- Cultural sovereignty is sacred; OCAP is enforced in code and practice
- We observe systems, never optimise people
- Sacred knowledge is hard-blocked, not warned
- Community authority carries the highest weight in decision paths

### Shareability Rules
- If a story cannot be told without harm, we do not tell it
- The signal stays internal until it is safe
- External sharing requires clarity about consent scope, cultural sensitivity, and purpose
- We do not trade story for attention

## Triple Bottom Line (Plus Culture)

ALMA tracks impact across four dimensions:

### Social Impact
- Community cohesion and connection
- Mental health and wellbeing
- Youth engagement and leadership
- Intergenerational relationships

### Environmental Impact
- Hectares restored or protected
- Threatened species populations
- Waste diverted from landfill
- Water quality improvement

### Economic Impact
- Jobs created in communities
- Revenue staying in communities (40% profit share)
- Community ownership value
- Financial sustainability

### Cultural Impact (Indigenous-Specific)
- Language preservation and use
- Cultural practices maintained or revived
- Traditional knowledge documented (with consent)
- Cultural authority and governance
- Connection to Country strengthened

---

## Measurement Approach

### Quantitative
- Platform analytics (usage, engagement)
- Financial records (revenue, profit sharing)
- Surveys (pre/post, satisfaction, NPS)
- Ecological monitoring (species counts, habitat quality)
- Employment data (hours, wages, retention)

### Qualitative
- Stories and testimonials
- Focus groups and listening circles
- Case studies (in-depth)
- Photo and video documentation
- Elder wisdom and reflection

### Most Significant Change (MSC)
- Stories of change collected
- Community selects most significant
- Dialogue about why it matters
- Patterns emerge over time
- Values and priorities revealed

---

> Field note: We do not measure people. We measure whether the system is learning.
