# Gold.Phone

**A telephone for stories that matter.**

Gold.Phone is an experimental communication tool designed for capturing and sharing community stories. Simple, intentional, and built for the stories that deserve more than a text message.

---

## Purpose

Modern communication is fast, disposable, and forgettable. Gold.Phone asks: what if we built something slower, more intentional, for the conversations that matter?

The tool enables:
- **Voice capture** - Stories told in voice, not typed
- **Simple interface** - As easy as making a phone call
- **Intentional sharing** - Choose who hears your story
- **Archive creation** - Stories preserved, not lost

---

## Design Principles

1. **Friction is good** - Slow down to be heard
2. **Voice over text** - Stories live in how they're told
3. **Consent first** - Nothing shared without permission
4. **Beautiful simplicity** - No features that don't serve story
