# The Harvest

**"Where time slows, roots deepen, and community grows"**

Community hub transforming a former nursery site in Witta into a model combining therapeutic horticulture, sustainable agriculture, and authentic community building.

---

## Location & Context

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Location** | 9 Gumland Drive, Witta QLD 4552 |
| **Heritage** | Former Green Harvest Organic Gardening Supplies (1993-2024, 31 years) |
| **Community** | Witta village (1,296 residents, median age 54+) |
| **Cultural Context** | German agricultural settlement (1887), UNESCO Biosphere |
| **Setting** | Sunshine Coast hinterland, Blackall Range |

---

## The Problem We're Addressing

Rural communities face:
- Isolation and aging demographics
- Youth exodus
- Limited mental health services
- Disconnection from land-based heritage
- German agricultural legacy at risk
- Organic gardening knowledge (31 years) potentially lost
- Commercial pressures on community spaces

---

## Our Approach

Honor heritage while building future. Therapeutic horticulture creates wellbeing pathways. CSA program builds food sovereignty. Seasonal gatherings strengthen community. Social enterprise model sustains space. Physical-over-digital, slow-over-fast, face-to-face connection.

---

## Key Programs

### Therapeutic Horticulture (with Cath Manuel)
- Person-centered, evidence-based approach
- Wellbeing through connection with nature
- Garden as healing space, not just food production
- Joy of gardening for mental health
- Supported by USC research

### Community Supported Agriculture (CSA)
- Seasonal harvest shares
- Community ownership of production
- Risk and reward shared
- Connection to food source
- Subscription model

### Seasonal Gatherings
- Shared meals celebrating harvest
- German heritage events
- Cultural exchange and learning
- Community building through food
- Storytelling and music

### Youth Engagement
- Gardening training and employment
- Pathways into agriculture
- Intergenerational knowledge transfer
- Rural youth retention

### Heritage Preservation
- Honoring German agricultural traditions
- Documenting organic gardening wisdom
- Celebrating 31-year Green Harvest legacy
- Cultural interpretation and education
