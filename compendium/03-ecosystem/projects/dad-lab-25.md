# Dad.Lab.25

**A space for fathers to connect, reflect, and grow.**

Dad.Lab.25 is a wellbeing program for fathers - a space where men can connect with each other, reflect on their roles as parents, and grow in ways that traditional support services don't offer.

---

## Purpose

Services for parents often focus on mothers, leaving fathers without spaces designed for them. Dad.Lab provides:

- **Connection** - Meeting other fathers facing similar challenges
- **Reflection** - Space to think about what kind of father you want to be
- **Skills** - Practical tools for parenting and relationships
- **Support** - A network that extends beyond the program

---

## Format

- Regular sessions (not one-off workshops)
- Facilitated by fathers with lived experience
- Action-focused: not just talking, doing
- Connection to broader support if needed
