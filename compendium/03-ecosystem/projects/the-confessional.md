# The Confessional

**A space where truth can be spoken without consequence.**

The Confessional is a storytelling installation that provides a safe, anonymous space for truth-telling. Inspired by the confessional booth, it invites people to share what they couldn't say elsewhere.

---

## Purpose

Some truths can't be spoken with your name attached. The fear of judgment, consequence, or exposure keeps important stories locked inside. The Confessional creates a container where:

- **Anonymity is guaranteed** - No names, no identification
- **Space is sacred** - Design that invites vulnerability
- **Stories are preserved** - With consent, truths become part of a collective archive
- **Connection through shared experience** - Others hear they're not alone

---

## How It Works

1. **Enter** - A private, enclosed space
2. **Record** - Voice capture, optionally distorted
3. **Choose** - Keep private, share anonymously, or contribute to archive
4. **Exit** - Leave lighter than you entered
