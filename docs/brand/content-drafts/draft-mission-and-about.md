# ACT Mission & About Page - Draft

## Mission Statement (Short)

A Curious Tractor is a regenerative innovation ecosystem partnering with marginalised—especially First Nations—communities to dismantle extractive systems. Like a tractor's power take-off, we transfer resources, knowledge, and capacity to community-led initiatives. We hand over the keys.

---

## Mission Statement (Expanded)

We cultivate seeds of justice, art, and shared governance from our working farm on Jinibara Country. Through deep listening, radical curiosity, tangible action, and transformative art, we're growing a world where communities own their narratives, land, and economic futures—while we design for our own obsolescence.

Social, cultural, environmental, and economic value stays in community hands. 40% of profits flow to community ownership. Every tool comes with a sunset clause.

## About Page

### Who We Are

A Curious Tractor exists at the intersection of regenerative land practice, social innovation, and cultural production. We're not consultants who parachute in with solutions. We're collaborators who show up, listen deeply, and transfer capacity to communities leading their own transformation.

Operating from Black Cockatoo Valley—a 150-acre conservation estate on Jinibara lands—we steward both working Country and a studio for systems change. The farm is our commons. The studio is our toolkit. Together, they demonstrate how land care, community ownership, and creative disruption can replace extractive systems with regenerative ones.

### How We Work: The LCAA Method

**Listen**
We sit in silence to take in knowledge. Before any action, we listen deeply—especially to voices often ignored. We pay attention to place, to people, to history, to ancestral teachers.

**Curiosity**
We lean into the unknown with open minds and hearts. We ask better questions, prototype boldly, test rigorously, and learn continuously. Curiosity is the soil from which innovation grows.

**Action**
We are makers who play and take chances. Through innovative technologies, compelling stories, and immersive art, we build tangible solutions alongside communities—not for them.

**Art**
We recognize art as the first form of revolution. By integrating creativity throughout our work, we challenge the status quo, provoke critical thinking, and inspire collective action.

### Our Values

**Radical Humility**
We don't have all the answers, but we're cultivating solutions together. No saviors here—just honest collaboration.

**Decentralised Power**
Communities lead; we support. Every tool we build has a sunset clause and forkable IP. When you're ready, we step back.

**Creativity as Disruption**
Revolution starts with imagination. Art opens new possibilities that policy alone never could.

**Uncomfortable Truth-telling**
We name extractive systems and work to dismantle them. Change requires getting our hands dirty with hard truths.

### What We Cultivate: Our Active Seeds

**Empathy Ledger** — Ethical storytelling platform where communities retain control over their narratives and share in the value created. Your story, your power, your profit.

**JusticeHub** — Open-source justice network where grassroots programs can fork proven models, access insights, and co-create culturally aligned governance. Justice by the community, for the community.

**Goods on Country** — Circular-economy venture co-designing essential products for remote communities while converting local waste into manufacturing inputs. Your waste, your wealth.

**Black Cockatoo Valley** — Our 150-acre regeneration estate combining eco-cottages, Indigenous land-care jobs, and biodiversity credits to finance habitat restoration. Country caring for people, people caring for Country.

**The Harvest** — Community-supported agriculture program and seasonal gatherings connecting local stewards to regenerative land practice and shared meals.

**Art** — Artworks, exhibitions, residencies, and commissions that use creativity as a tool for systems change and cultural transformation.

### Our Promise

We're building a dual-entity structure—a charitable foundation paired with a mission-locked trading company—that ensures our purpose can never be sold or diluted. 40% of profits flow directly to community ownership. The other 60% reinvests in scaling what works.

This isn't charity. It's not traditional business either. It's a regenerative ecosystem where profit serves purpose, where communities co-own the solutions, and where our success is measured by how well we prepare the ground for our own obsolescence.
