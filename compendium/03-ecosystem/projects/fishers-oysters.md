# Fishers Oysters

**Restoring oyster reefs, restoring sovereignty.**

Fishers Oysters is an Indigenous-led aquaculture enterprise that combines ecological restoration with economic sovereignty. By restoring oyster reefs that were decimated by colonial extraction, the project rebuilds both ecosystem and community self-determination.

---

## Purpose

Australia's oyster reefs were 99% destroyed in the 200 years since colonisation. For coastal Indigenous communities, this wasn't just environmental loss - it was loss of food source, cultural practice, and economic independence. Fishers Oysters responds by:

- **Reef restoration** - Rebuilding oyster habitat
- **Indigenous enterprise** - Economic sovereignty through sustainable business
- **Cultural connection** - Reconnecting to traditional fishing practices
- **Environmental healing** - Oysters as ecosystem restorers

---

## Why Oysters Matter

Oyster reefs are ecological powerhouses:
- Filter water, improving quality
- Provide habitat for fish and marine life
- Protect shorelines from erosion
- Sequester carbon
- Support sustainable food production

When Indigenous communities lead their restoration, ecological and social healing happen together.
