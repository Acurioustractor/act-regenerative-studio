# 10x10 Community Capital Leadership Retreat

**Ten leaders, ten days, growing community capital together.**

The 10x10 Retreat brings together ten community leaders for an intensive period of learning, connection, and practice development around community capital and shared enterprise.

---

## Purpose

Community capital - the networks, trust, and shared resources that make communities strong - doesn't grow by accident. This retreat provides:

- **Deep learning** - Extended time for ideas to develop
- **Peer connection** - Building relationships that outlast the retreat
- **Practice development** - Creating tools and approaches to take home
- **Shared enterprise** - Exploring models for community-owned business

---

## Format

- **10 participants** from diverse community leadership contexts
- **10 days** of immersive learning and practice
- **Location** - The Harvest and surrounding community
- **Outputs** - Action plans, connections, shared tools
