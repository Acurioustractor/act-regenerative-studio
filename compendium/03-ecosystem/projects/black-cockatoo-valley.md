# Black Cockatoo Valley

**Restoring habitat, restoring connection.**

Black Cockatoo Valley is a land regeneration project focused on restoring habitat for black cockatoos and other native species, while creating spaces for community connection to Country.

---

## Purpose

Black cockatoos are environmental indicators - where they thrive, ecosystems are healthy. Where they decline, something is wrong. This project combines:

- **Habitat restoration** - Planting food sources and nesting habitat
- **Community involvement** - Volunteers and neighbours caring for land
- **Cultural connection** - Black cockatoos hold significance in many Indigenous cultures
- **Learning site** - Demonstrating regenerative land management

---

## Why Black Cockatoos?

Three species of black cockatoo are found in the region, all under threat:
- **Red-tailed Black Cockatoo** - Endangered
- **Glossy Black Cockatoo** - Vulnerable
- **Yellow-tailed Black Cockatoo** - Near threatened

Restoring their habitat means restoring whole ecosystems.
