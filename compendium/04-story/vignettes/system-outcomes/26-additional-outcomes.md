# Collective Impact: Additional Outcome Stories

> "Every number is a person. Every outcome is a story. Aggregated, they tell us what's possible when we do this work right."

---

## Story Summary

This vignette serves as a collection point for additional outcome stories as they are documented. The 28 stories catalogued in this compendium represent a fraction of the impact being generated across ACT programs and partnerships. As more stories are captured and consented for sharing, they will be added here or elevated to their own vignettes.

### Emerging Outcome Themes

**Employment Pathways**
Young people engaged with ACT programs are finding pathways to employment at higher rates than comparison groups. These aren't just "first jobs"—they're meaningful employment that aligns with interests, utilizes skills, and provides genuine career development.

**Family Reconnection**
Some of the most powerful outcomes can't be measured in traditional metrics. Family members who haven't spoken in years rebuilding relationships. Parents regaining custody after demonstrating stability. Extended family networks reactivating to support young people.

**Housing Stability**
The foundation for everything else. Young people moving from homelessness or unstable housing to sustainable living situations. Not just crisis accommodation—genuine homes where they can build futures.

**Justice System Diversion**
Young people who would have cycled through detention are instead receiving support that addresses underlying causes. The cost savings are significant, but more importantly, young lives aren't being derailed by criminal records.

**Cultural Revival**
Language being spoken again. Ceremonies being attended. Young people identifying proudly with culture that had been suppressed or unavailable to them. Elders finding purpose in passing on knowledge to eager learners.

**Community Leadership**
Young people who were once "clients" becoming mentors, advocates, and leaders. The transformation from service recipient to community contributor represents a fundamental shift in identity and possibility.
