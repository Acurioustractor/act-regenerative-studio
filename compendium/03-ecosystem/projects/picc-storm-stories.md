# PICC Storm Stories

**Community-led documentation of Cyclone Kirrily impact and recovery**

When Cyclone Kirrily hit Palm Island in early 2024, the community faced not just physical recovery but a familiar challenge: external organizations wanting to document their experience. Storm Stories changed the pattern—community members documented their own stories, on their own terms.

---

## Purpose

Disasters bring media attention. Journalists and photographers arrive, capture dramatic images, file stories, and leave. The community becomes content for someone else's narrative.

Storm Stories took a different approach. Instead of outside documentation, PICC partnered with ACT to build community capacity for self-documentation. Local community members received training in photography, videography, and interview techniques. More importantly, they learned protocols for story consent and sharing—ensuring community control over their own narratives.

The project demonstrated that data sovereignty isn't just about databases. It's about who holds the camera, who asks the questions, and who decides what happens with the answers.
