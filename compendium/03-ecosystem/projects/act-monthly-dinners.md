# ACT Monthly Dinners

**Breaking bread, building connection, crafting shared story.**

ACT Monthly Dinners are regular gatherings at The Harvest where people come together to eat, listen, and contribute to ACT's evolving story. Not a meeting. Not a presentation. A meal where conversation shapes what we become.

---

## Purpose

The best insights don't come from reports. They come from dinner conversation - the kind that happens when people are fed, comfortable, and given permission to speak honestly.

Monthly dinners provide:
- **Shared meal** - Food grown and prepared with care
- **Listening space** - Structured conversation that surfaces insight
- **Community voice** - Participants shape ACT's direction
- **Connection** - Relationships that extend beyond the evening

---

## How It Works

1. **Gather** - People arrive at The Harvest
2. **Eat** - Shared meal, often from the farm
3. **Listen** - Structured conversation on themes that matter
4. **Contribute** - Everyone's voice shapes the story
5. **Connect** - Relationships continue beyond the evening
