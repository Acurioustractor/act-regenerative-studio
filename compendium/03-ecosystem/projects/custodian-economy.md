# Custodian Economy

**Exploring economic models where communities own and steward their resources.**

Custodian Economy is ACT's research and practice work developing economic models that centre community ownership and stewardship. It asks: what does an economy look like when it's designed to serve custodians, not extractors?

---

## Purpose

The dominant economic model extracts value from communities, concentrates it in few hands, and leaves places depleted. Custodian Economy explores alternatives:

- **Community ownership** - Assets held by those who use them
- **Stewardship over extraction** - Value that regenerates, not depletes
- **Place-based economies** - Economic activity that strengthens local connection
- **Indigenous economic principles** - Learning from systems that sustained for millennia

---

## Research Areas

1. **Land trusts and commons** - How to hold land for community benefit
2. **Worker and community cooperatives** - Ownership models that share value
3. **Indigenous economic sovereignty** - Economic self-determination
4. **Circular and regenerative economies** - Systems that restore rather than deplete
