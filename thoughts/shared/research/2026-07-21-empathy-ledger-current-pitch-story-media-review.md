# Current Empathy Ledger pitch, story and media review

## Research question

What does the current Empathy Ledger work say and show, especially across the pitch, story system, public experience, images and governance, and how should the ACT field story represent it?

## Summary

The current canonical explanation is substantially stronger and more complete than the earlier consent-tool framing.

Empathy Ledger is **community knowledge infrastructure**, not an AI product. Its core public proposition is: **Stories are not content. They are relationships.** The current product model is a five-stage river:

1. Source
2. Boundary
3. Review
4. Use
5. Return

The platform already expresses this model through story writing, cinematic private review, storyteller-owned visibility, named use records, return agreements, correction and withdrawal, and revocable syndication.

The current public narrative also uses a three-part institutional model:

- **The Ledger** keeps story, media, source, permission, decision and provenance connected.
- **The evidence engine** turns approved knowledge into traceable themes, outcomes, reports and claims.
- **Reciprocity** keeps uses, benefits, obligations and return visible over time.

The same underlying record is presented through bounded views for the storyteller, community authority, platform steward and approved audience. This is not role-based decoration. It is how the product avoids making public access the default shape of knowledge.
