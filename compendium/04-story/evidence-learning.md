# Evidence & Learning

Storytelling is infrastructure for change, not marketing. Stories are evidence because they carry context, harm risk, and authority.

---

## ACT's Approach to Impact

### Community-Defined Metrics

Not ACT-imposed:
- Communities define what success looks like
- Metrics co-created, not prescribed
- Qualitative valued equally with quantitative
- Story as valid form of evidence
- Cultural measures (not just Western economic)

### Process

1. **Listening phase:** What matters to community?
2. **Co-design:** How do we know if it's working?
3. **Baseline:** Where are we now?
4. **Track:** Monitor with community participation
5. **Review:** Regular reflection and course correction
6. **Celebrate:** Honor progress and learning

## Shareability Matrix

Content is tagged with shareability levels:

| Level | Description | Use |
|-------|-------------|-----|
| **INTERNAL ONLY** | Team only, not for external sharing | Internal planning, learning |
| **EXTERNAL-LITE** | Can share with permission/review | Partner communications, context-aware sharing |
| **EXTERNAL** | Public, consented for broad sharing | Website, publications, media |

### Rules

- External sharing requires clarity about consent scope, cultural sensitivity, and purpose
- We do not trade story for attention
- If a story cannot be told without harm, we do not tell it
- We wait and listen again when uncertain

## Reporting Accountability

### Who We Report To

**Communities (Primary Accountability)**
- Quarterly impact reviews (open meetings)
- Annual celebration and reflection
- Real-time dashboards (community access)
- Story-based reporting (not just numbers)
- Community decides what's shared externally

**Funders & Partners**
- Grant reports (required formats)
- Impact summaries (quantitative + qualitative)
- Learning and challenges (honest assessment)
- Community voice included
- ROI and efficiency metrics

**Public & Movement**
- Annual impact report (published openly)
- Blog posts and case studies
- Presentations and speaking
- Academic publications (with community consent)
- Media coverage (strategic)

## Reporting Formats

### Dashboards
- Real-time platform metrics
- Environmental monitoring data
- Financial transparency (revenue, profit sharing)
- Program participation
- Community access (not just ACT internal)

### Stories
- Multimedia storytelling (video, photo, audio)
- Case studies (in-depth, consented)
- Testimonials (attributed with permission)
- Community voices centered
- Context and nuance preserved

### Data Visualizations
- Infographics (accessible, shareable)
- Maps (environmental, network, geographic)
- Timelines (project evolution)
- Impact pathways (logic models)
- Comparative (before/after, with/without)

## Learning & Adaptation

### Continuous Improvement

**What We Track:**
- What's working (celebrate and replicate)
- What's not (course correct or exit)
- Unintended consequences (positive and negative)
- Assumptions challenged
- Community feedback

**How We Adapt:**
- Quarterly review and planning
- Rapid iteration on tactics
- Thoughtful pivots on strategy
- Community input on changes
- Transparent about why

### Failure as Learning

**Compost, Don't Hide:**
- Public sharing of what didn't work
- Honest assessment of mistakes
- Community voice on failures
- Learning documented for others
- No shame in admitting wrong approach

**Example Failures to Share:**
- Tech solutions that were too complex
- Programs that didn't match community needs
- Partnerships that weren't reciprocal
- Funding models that created dependency
- Timelines that were too ambitious

## Participatory Evaluation

### Community as Evaluators
- Community members trained in evaluation
- Community defines success criteria
- Community interprets findings
- Community decides what to share publicly
- Community owns evaluation data

### Most Significant Change (MSC)
- Stories of change collected
- Community selects most significant
- Dialogue about why it matters
- Patterns emerge over time
- Values and priorities revealed

## Evidence Types

| Type | Description | Validity |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Outcome data** | Measurable changes (recidivism, employment, etc.) | Quantitative proof |
| **Narrative** | Stories carrying context and meaning | Qualitative evidence |
| **Service demand** | External referrals, requests | System trust signal |
| **Engagement** | Participation, retention | Program relevance |
| **Community voice** | What community says matters | Authority signal |

---

> Field note: Metrics support learning, but do not lead it. Stories are evidence because they carry context, harm risk, and authority.
