# ACT Compendium 2026 Visual Briefs

Use these briefs to build diagrams and graphics for the compendium. Keep visuals consent-safe, place-led, and grounded.

## V1. ACT Core Model map

Purpose: show the full system in one view.

Core elements:

- Land practice (Black Cockatoo Valley, Jinibara Country)
- Enterprise (Harvest + Goods)
- Story and evidence (Empathy Ledger)
- Governance and learning (ALMA)
- Method loop (LCAA)

Layout idea:

- Center: ACT Core Model
- Four anchors around the center with arrows between:
  - Land practice <-> Enterprise
  - Enterprise <-> Story and evidence
  - Story and evidence <-> Governance and learning
  - Governance and learning <-> Land practice
- LCAA loop as a ring around the core.

Key callouts (short):

- Conservation-first baseline
- Community ownership
- Consent-first storytelling
- Adaptive learning for accountability

## V2. LCAA loop with 2026 examples

Purpose: show the method in motion, not as a slogan.

Quadrants:

- Listen: Elders speak on storm response
- Curiosity: prototypes for beds and washing machines
- Action: Harvest enterprise hub running small operations
- Art: story vignette that reframes impact as culture

Output: circular diagram with one short example per quadrant.

## V3. ALMA reporting loop

Purpose: make ALMA legible and repeatable for reporting.

Loop:

1) Story (consented)
2) Signal (ALMA lens)
3) Shift (what changes)
4) Scope (what can be shared)

Include one real vignette as a worked example.

## V4. Enterprise pathway loops

Purpose: show local capability and maintenance as core value.

Loop A: Goods

- Co-design
- Prototype
- Manufacturing
- Maintenance + repair
- Local ownership
- Back to co-design

Loop B: Harvest

- Local makers
- Markets + workshops
- Skills + income
- Community connection
- New enterprise tests
- Back to makers

## V5. Ecosystem map

Purpose: show outputs vs expressions and how they connect.

Layers:

- Outputs (Empathy Ledger, JusticeHub, Goods, Harvest, Black Cockatoo Valley, ACT Farm)
- Expressions (residencies, events, artworks, harvest shares, programs)
- Governance (ALMA + LCAA)

Style: simple nodes and connectors, no heavy gradients.

## V6. Consent and data flow map

Purpose: show Empathy Ledger as source of truth and consent gate.

Flow:

Story capture -> Consent review -> Empathy Ledger -> ALMA signals -> Reporting and product decisions

Callouts:

- Consent is explicit and revocable
- Sacred knowledge is blocked
- System-level learning only

## V7. Project snapshot grid (optional)

Purpose: quick scan of active projects.

Format:

- 3 or 4 columns
- 1 line per project (name + micro description)
- Tags for place and focus area

## V8. Impact learning timeline (optional)

Purpose: show learning cadence across the year.

Format:

- Q1 to Q4 timeline
- One ALMA checkpoint per quarter
- One community review point per half-year
