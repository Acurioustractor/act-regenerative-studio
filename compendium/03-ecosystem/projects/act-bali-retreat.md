# ACT stop, revive, thrive in Bali

**Rest, reset, and renew.**

The ACT Bali retreat is a rest and renewal program - time away from the demands of work to reset capacity, refresh strategy, and renew energy for the work ahead.

---

## Purpose

Burnout is an occupational hazard in social impact work. This retreat provides:

- **Rest** - Genuine downtime away from work
- **Reset** - Space to reflect on priorities
- **Renewal** - Coming back with energy, not depletion
- **Strategy** - Time to think about direction
