# Diagrama

**Learning from Spain's therapeutic justice revolution.**

Diagrama is a partnership with the Diagrama Foundation in Spain, one of the world's most successful examples of therapeutic approaches to youth justice. The partnership brings their learning to Australian contexts while respecting the need for local adaptation.

---

## Purpose

Spain has achieved what Australia struggles to imagine: youth detention that actually rehabilitates, recidivism rates that plummet, young people who emerge better than when they entered. Diagrama is how we learn from that success.

The partnership enables:
- **Site visits** - ACT teams learning directly from Diagrama practice
- **Knowledge exchange** - Sharing methodology and evidence
- **Australian adaptation** - Translating learning for local contexts
- **Reference practice** - Demonstrating what's possible

## Key Learnings from Diagrama

1. **Therapeutic environment** - Physical spaces that heal, not harm
2. **Staff relationships** - Ratio and training that enables connection
3. **Education focus** - Every young person continues learning
4. **Family involvement** - Keeping connections strong
5. **Transition planning** - Support that continues beyond release
