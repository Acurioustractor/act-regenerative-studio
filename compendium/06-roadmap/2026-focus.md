# 2026 Focus Areas

This year is about balance: land stewardship, studio innovation, and enterprise maturity moving in step.

> "Pace is a form of ethics. We move at the speed of trust, maintenance, and land capacity."

---

## The 2026 Balance

```
     LAND STEWARDSHIP
           ↕
  STUDIO INNOVATION ← → ENTERPRISE MATURITY
```

Every project should explain:
1. How it strengthens land practice
2. How it advances responsible innovation
3. How it builds enterprise sustainability

---

## Focus Area 1: Land Stewardship with Shared Governance

### Goals
- Keep conservation-first practice as the baseline
- Prepare co-stewardship groundwork (relationship, protocol, readiness)
- Scale land programs to land capacity and community readiness

### Actions
- Continue Nature Refuge designation and habitat restoration
- Run residencies and activities at sustainable pace
- Deepen Traditional Owner engagement
- Document pathways for community co-stewardship

### Success Looks Like
- Land capacity respected (not exceeded)
- Stronger relationships with Traditional Owners
- Clear governance handover pathway defined
- Habitat health improving

---

## Focus Area 2: Innovation Studio and Product Work

### Goals
- Build tools that serve community ownership, not dependence
- Prioritise clarity, simplicity, and handover readiness
- Use stories and evidence to guide product direction

### Actions
- Strengthen Empathy Ledger consent and Elder review
- Deepen ALMA integration across platforms
- Keep OCAP enforcement non-negotiable
- Document everything for handover

### Success Looks Like
- Tools that communities can fork and run independently
- ALMA signals visible across ecosystem
- Clear documentation for all major systems
- Community satisfaction with products

## Focus Area 3: The Harvest as Grounded Enterprise (Witta)

### Goals
- Support local makers and micro-enterprise
- Build repeatable operations for workshops, markets, venue
- Use enterprise as testing ground for sustainable practice
- Prioritise sustainable, community-led, innovation-ready enterprise

### Actions
- Formalize therapeutic horticulture partnership with Cath Manuel
- Establish CSA program structure
- Build seasonal event calendar
- Create sustainable business model

### Success Looks Like
- Programs running with positive community feedback
- Revenue on track to 70% earned by Year 3
- Heritage documented and active
- Youth engaged in programs

## Focus Area 5: Story and Evidence

### Goals
- Treat storytelling as infrastructure for change, not marketing
- Protect consent and cultural protocols in every output
- Use evidence to learn, not to extract
- Anchor impact learning in ALMA and Empathy Ledger

### Actions
- Strengthen consent workflows and Elder review
- Integrate ALMA signals across all projects
- Build story vignette library with proper consent
- Use evidence to inform product and enterprise decisions

### Success Looks Like
- Zero consent violations
- Stories driving learning and improvement
- Community voice centered in all communications
- ALMA-informed decision-making

## Cross-Cutting Principles for 2026

### Country Sets the Pace
If the creek is low or the grass is tired, we slow down. Capacity is a land decision before it is a calendar decision.

### Identity Before Product
We start with belonging, not features. Events are culture, not marketing.

### Build for Handover
Documentation and training are deliverables, not extras. If we cannot hand it over, we have not finished the work.

### Evidence is Story
We do not profile people. We track system-level signals, learn from consented stories, and change practice in response.
