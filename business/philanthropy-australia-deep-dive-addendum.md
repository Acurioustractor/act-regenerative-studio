## Membership & Better Giving Hub

Philanthropy Australia (PA) runs three named tiers, with ENGAGE split in two: **CONNECT A$700+GST, ENGAGE Individual A$1,700+GST, ENGAGE Organisation A$3,500+GST, IMPACT A$7,500+GST**, all billed yearly (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/become-a-member/; flyer src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Membership-flyer-May-2025-2.pdf). PA also describes a legacy "five levels of membership" (incl. New Gen) alongside the simplified three (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/about-us/who-we-are/).

**Per-tier benefits matrix** (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/member-benefits-comparison/ and the flyer PDF):

| Benefit | CONNECT | ENGAGE Indiv | ENGAGE Org | IMPACT |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fortnightly enewsletter | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Better Giving Hub (resource portal) | ✓ (capped at 10) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Sector Recruitment job board | ✓ (capped at 2/yr) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Alliance Magazine subscription | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Discount on key events | 10% | 25% (capped at one) | 25% | 25% |
| Professional Development & Networking | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Staff Support (PA staffer helps you connect) | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Input into Strategic Initiatives | — | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Visibility & Influence (speaking, articles) | — | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Priority Event Access | — | — | — | ✓ |
| Invitations to VIP events | — | — | — | ✓ |

The three caps map to: Better Giving Hub (CONNECT team, capped at 10), event discount (ENGAGE Individual, capped at one), job board (capped at 2/year) (src: flyer PDF; src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/faqs-updated-membership-tiers/).

**Voting rights.** CONNECT carries the footnote "*Excludes member voting rights. See our Membership policy for details*" (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/become-a-member/). By exclusion, paid ENGAGE (Individual + Org) and IMPACT tiers carry voting rights; PA's governance materials state voting members can be elected to the Board (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/about-us/governance/; src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/about-us/publications/board-nomination-forms/). PA is a company limited by guarantee, so voting is exercised at the AGM (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/about-us/governance/).

**Directory listing is NOT tier-gated.** It is absent from the benefits matrix and flyer. PA lists 800+ member organisations publicly, but this is opt-in for members generally ("members listed... having agreed to appear there"), not a paid differentiator (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/about-us/members/). The "Proud Member" logo is likewise open to all members (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/about-us/logo-for-member-use/).

**Better Giving Hub contents.** The Hub is the members' online resource portal across all tiers, holding publications, research reports, webinars, best-practice tools, member-news uploads, and **Foundation Maps** (who funds what/where) (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/tools-resources/better-giving-hub/; src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/guidance-and-tools/grantmaking-resources/direct-giving/). It lives at bgh.philanthropy.org.au — a login-gated Salesforce Experience Cloud site (bettergivinghub.org 301-redirects to it). The /s/memberships page returns 200 in-browser but 403 to automated fetch, so the full Hub catalogue is members-only and not publicly extractable.

## Events, training & awards

**Conference 2026.** Runs Tue 8–Thu 10 Sep 2026 in Brisbane, theme "Mobilising Generosity, Shaping the Future," 1,000+ attendees expected, with fringe events Mon 7 Sep (Masterclass Monday) and Fri 11 Sep (Field Trip Friday) (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/whats-on/philanthropy-australia-conference-2026/). The 3-day core-pass pricing, from the EventsAir portal, is tiered by membership then early-bird vs general (src: https://philanthropy.eventsair.com/philanthropy-australia-conference-2026/genconf):

- **Impact / Engage member:** A$1,935 early bird / A$2,175 general
- **Connect member:** A$2,322 early bird / A$2,610 general
- **Non-member:** A$2,580 early bird / A$2,900 general

All prices include GST; a 2.25% booking fee is added. The member-vs-non-member gap is roughly A$645 early bird / A$725 general for the top member tier. Early-bird registrations close 5 June 2026. Scholarships cover full core-conference registration; applications closed 16 April 2026 (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/news-and-stories/conference-2026-general-early-bird-and-scholarships-open/).

**Webinars / masterclasses — mixed model.** A standalone full-day evaluation masterclass (4 June 2025) was open to all ("Philanthropy Australia or LEAF Network membership not required"), priced by funder size at A$400 / A$500 / A$600 +GST for small/medium/large funders (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/whats-on/transforming-grantmaking-through-evaluation-impact-measurement/). Membership confers "member-only discounts for events," with Engage/Impact getting exclusive invites and Impact getting VIP events; the page does not state webinars are free for members (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/become-a-member/).

**LEAF Network** (Learning & Evaluation for Australian Funders). Member-driven MEL community, formerly "Phil Eval Network," 100+ members as of 2024; access includes a Slack portal (resource library, discussions, events, peer call-outs). It "regularly runs masterclasses" as 3–4 hour topic deep-dives, led by Liz Branigan, Director Knowledge & Practice (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/about-us/leaf-network/; src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/about-us/our-team/liz-branigan/).

**Deep Listening Communities of Practice.** In 2024, PA ran two CoPs under LEAF — each 6 weeks, weekly 1-hour Zoom plus an online discussion space, with all participants getting a free copy of Oscar Trimboli's *How to Listen* (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/news-and-stories/listening-questioning-and-acting-to-drive-better-philanthropy/).

**Peer-network cadence.** Topic peer-networks run ~monthly, 1-hour, 12–1pm AEST virtual (e.g. Social Enterprise 1 June, Family/"Connecting Generations" 2 June, Arts 4 June 2026) (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/whats-on/). State networks: QLD "bi-monthly gatherings"; SA/WA "regular meetings"; Arts "regular formal meetings and informal gatherings." Engage and Impact tiers may join relevant networks; FGII and some funder networks don't require membership (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/about-us/peer-networks/).

**Australian Philanthropy Awards 2026.** Not held in 2025; most recent ceremony 2024. Site states only "Stay tuned for more information about the 2026 Australian Philanthropy Awards" — no 2026 dates, nominations closed. Nine categories exist (Inclusion, Innovation, Collaboration, Leadership, Environmental & Climate, Community, International, First Nations, Eve Mahlab AO Gender-wise) (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/about-us/australian-philanthropy-awards/).

## Research, advocacy & blueprint

**Strategy 2033.** Released 6 March 2026 for PA's 50th anniversary, framing PA as "ecosystem builders," vision "Strengthen giving for a more generous, just and sustainable future," tagline "Mobilising Generosity. Shaping the Future" (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/about-us/publications/strategy-2033/). It commits PA to four actions: **Convene** (connect/engage members), **Amplify** (insights and knowledge exchange), **Influence** (use of diverse resources for impact), **Advocate** (policy outcomes). The published page carries **no numeric target** — it is action-framed; "$5B by 2030" stays attached to the older Blueprint, with secondary coverage only loosely saying Strategy 2033 "aligns with" that roadmap (src: same).

**The Blueprint's 10 initiatives.** PROTECT: (1) maintain refundable franking credits; (2) maintain uncapped tax deductions; (3) preserve an effective ACNC regulator; (4) protect charity advocacy; (5) support charities' work. ENHANCE: (6) remove barriers to excess-super donations (kill the 15% + Medicare penalty on super bequests); (7) reform DGR (≈half of charities ineligible); (8) introduce a "Living Legacy" trust; (9) champion diverse giving stories; (10) develop a research agenda — plus place-based/community-foundation red-tape cuts and a UHNW giving report (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/our-impact/a-blueprint-to-grow-structured-giving/). Baseline: double structured giving from **$2.5B (2020) to $5B by 2030**. No per-initiative progress status is published.

**A Coalition of the Giving** remains under-specified. The Blueprint launch (21 Apr 2021, then-CEO Jack Heath) describes it only as a planned "cross-sector group" requiring "commitments of support and funding… including from government" — no member organisations are named in any accessible PA page (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/news-and-stories/plan-to-double-australian-giving-starts-with-a-blueprint/).

**Flagship research.** PA leans on the **JBWere Bequest Report** (John McLeod, Aug 2024): ~$150B inherited per year but only **$1.3B to charity**, ~**6.5% of wills** include a charitable gift, and a **$5.4 trillion** 20-year wealth transfer (up from the PC's 2017 $3.5T) (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/news-and-stories/150bn-will-be-inherited-this-year-jbwere-bequest-report/). JBWere data also underpins the claim that PAFs could grow from ~2,000 to **50,000+** (PAFs hold ~$10B corpus, distribute >$500M/yr) and that reforms could add **$5.6B** of giving over 25 years (src: https://www.jbwere.com.au/content/dam/jbwere/documents/campaigns/JBWere-Submission-Giving-Fund-Reforms.pdf). PA's "Giving Trends and Opportunities" notes donation dollars rising while the share of people who give falls, citing the $2.6T baby-boomer wealth transfer (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/learn-about-philanthropy/giving-trends-and-opportunities/). Australia's giving is **0.81% of GDP** vs NZ 1.84% / US 2.1% (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Philanthropy-Australia-submission-Full-Report-Final-5-May-2023.pdf).

**Submissions PA backs.** On the PC draft, PA backed DGR expansion (≈25,000→30–40,000 charities), super bequests, a First Nations-controlled foundation, corporate-donation transparency and bequest reporting — but said the draft gave "no roadmap" and missed a National Giving Campaign, a tax-return giving nudge and Living Legacy Trusts (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/news-and-stories/positive-but-more-to-do-on-double-giving-our-response-to-productivity-commissions-draft-findings/). On the final *Future Foundations for Giving*, PA (CEO Maree Sidey) welcomed the DGR overhaul ("not fit for purpose"), fundraising harmonisation, "Indigenous Philanthropy Connections" and reframing ancillary funds as "giving funds"; it was disappointed super bequests, the tax nudge and a government-funded giving campaign were not recommended (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/news-and-stories/advocacy-update-productivity-commissions-future-foundations-for-giving-report/). For the **Arts & Cultural Philanthropy Inquiry**, PA backed a public statement of government-vs-philanthropy roles, the PC's DGR reform, no-cost super bequests to arts bodies, and continued Creative Australia capacity-building funding (src: https://www.philanthropy.org.au/news-and-stories/strengthening-arts-and-cultural-philanthropy-pas-submission-to-the-australian-parliaments-committee-inquiry/).
