# Oonchiumpa

**Intensive, culturally-grounded support for young people navigating crisis**

Oonchiumpa provides wraparound support for young people in the Northern Territory who are at the intersection of multiple systems—justice, child protection, education, housing. Rather than treating symptoms in isolation, Oonchiumpa addresses the whole person, with cultural reconnection at the center of everything.

---

## Purpose

Young people in crisis don't need more appointments. They need relationships, stability, and connection to culture and community. Oonchiumpa provides intensive, persistent support that follows young people through whatever challenges they face—at any time of day or night.

The program operates on a simple premise: young people make good decisions when good options are available. When housing is stable, when relationships are healthy, when cultural identity is strong, when pathways to education and employment are visible—young people thrive. Oonchiumpa creates those conditions.

What makes Oonchiumpa distinctive is its refusal to give up. The young people referred to the program have often exhausted other services. Oonchiumpa doesn't see them as "hard cases" but as young people who haven't yet received the right support. That belief shapes everything.

## Key Activities

1. **24/7 Support** - Crisis response at any hour, relationship-based engagement
2. **Cultural Programming** - On-Country trips, Elder connections, identity work
3. **Practical Support** - Housing, transport, education, employment pathways
4. **Family Work** - Reconnection where safe, alternative support where not
5. **System Navigation** - Coordinated response across justice, education, health
