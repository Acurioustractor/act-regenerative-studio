# MingaMinga Rangers

**Growing rangers, growing capability, caring for Country.**

MingaMinga Rangers is a ranger pathway program supporting Indigenous communities to care for Country while building local employment and capability. The program combines land management with economic opportunity.

---

## Purpose

Ranger programs are one of Australia's most successful models for combining:

- **Land care** - Active management of Country
- **Employment** - Meaningful work on Country
- **Knowledge transfer** - Elders teaching young people
- **Economic development** - Community-controlled enterprise
- **Cultural practice** - Traditional knowledge in action

MingaMinga Rangers supports communities to build and sustain their own ranger programs.
