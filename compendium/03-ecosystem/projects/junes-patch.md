# June's Patch

**Growing food, growing connection, growing wellbeing.**

June's Patch is a land-based program that uses gardening and food production as a pathway to wellbeing. Named in honour of connection to country and care, the program brings people together around the simple, grounding work of growing food.

---

## Purpose

For many people experiencing disconnection - from community, from purpose, from themselves - the path back isn't through a service or a program. It's through dirt, seeds, and the patience of waiting for something to grow.

June's Patch provides:
- **Hands in soil** - Physical, grounding work that quiets the mind
- **Shared purpose** - Working alongside others toward something tangible
- **Food sovereignty** - Growing what you eat, knowing where it comes from
- **Seasonal rhythm** - Learning to work with nature's pace, not against it
