# ACT Compendium 2026 Visual Specs (V1, V3, V5)

These are final copy + layout specs for the first three visuals. Keep consent-first, place-led, and grounded in LCAA + ALMA.

## V1. ACT Core Model Map

Title:
ACT Core Model

Subtitle:
Land practice, enterprise, story, and governance held in one system.

Layout:

- Format: A4 landscape (print) + 16:9 (screen) + 4:5 (social).
- Center: "ACT Core Model" in a rounded lozenge.
- Four anchors around center with clockwise flow arrows:
  - Land Practice
  - Enterprise
  - Story + Evidence
  - Governance + Learning
- LCAA loop as a thin ring around the center (Listen -> Curiosity -> Action -> Art).

Anchor labels + copy:

Land Practice
- Black Cockatoo Valley (Jinibara Country)
- Conservation-first baseline
- Community co-stewardship readiness

Enterprise
- The Harvest (Witta) + Goods
- Local capability and maintenance
- Community ownership and value return

Story + Evidence
- Empathy Ledger
- Consent-first storytelling
- Lived experience as evidence

Governance + Learning
- ALMA (Adaptive Learning for Meaningful Accountability)
- System-level learning, no profiling
- Community authority as the highest weight

LCAA ring micro-copy:

- Listen: start with place and people
- Curiosity: prototype and learn together
- Action: build with partners
- Art: translate change into culture

Footer callout:
Outputs carry infrastructure. Expressions carry culture.

Palette + type:

- Colors: Soil #5A4638, Clay #B07A5A, Sage #6A7D5C, Ochre #C89B3C, Stone #E8E2D8, Charcoal #2E2B27.
- Type: Headings in Fraunces (600), body in Work Sans (400/500).

Diagram showing ACT Core Model with land practice, enterprise, story, and governance connected around an LCAA loop.

## V3. ALMA Reporting Loop

Title:
ALMA Reporting Loop

Subtitle:
Story -> signal -> shift -> scope.

Layout:

- Format: A4 portrait + 16:9.
- Circular loop with four nodes.
- Center label: "Adaptive Learning for Meaningful Accountability".
- Optional side panel with one example vignette.

Node copy:

1) Story
- A consented lived experience
- Voice held by community

2) Signal
- System-level pattern
- No individual profiling

3) Shift
- What we change next
- Practical action, not theory

4) Scope
- What can be shared
- Internal vs external-lite vs external

Side-panel example (use one vignette):

Example story: Community Innovation (Beds + Washing Machines)
Signal: local repair and maintenance is as critical as production.
Shift: invest in maintenance and training pathways.
Scope: EXTERNAL-LITE.

Footer callout:
Consent is explicit, ongoing, and revocable.

Alt text (draft):
Circular ALMA loop with four steps and a consent-first example.
