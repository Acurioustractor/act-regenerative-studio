# Farmhand & AI Strategy

Farmhand and ALMA are internal tools for coordination and learning. They matter only if they enforce cultural sovereignty and avoid extraction.

> "Intelligence without consent is just extraction. We would rather be slower than wrong."

---

## Core Philosophy

AI agents must align with ALMA and Empathy Ledger consent. The point is not automation at all costs, but ethical intelligence that protects community sovereignty.

### Non-Negotiables

| Principle | Implementation |
|-----------|----------------|
| Cultural sovereignty is sacred | OCAP enforced in code and practice |
| We observe systems, never optimise people | No individual profiling or scoring |
| Sacred knowledge is hard-blocked | Not warned—blocked entirely |
| Community authority carries highest weight | In all decision paths |

---

## Farmhand Role

Farmhand turns messy inputs into clear next actions across the Knowledge Hub.

### What Farmhand Does
- Turn messy inputs into clear tasks and next actions
- Protect consent and cultural boundaries in summaries and outputs
- Connect LCAA steps to real work without inflating claims
- Keep ALMA signals visible without profiling individuals

### What Farmhand Does NOT Do
- Override community voice
- Profile individuals
- Rank or score people
- Make decisions without human review
- Access sacred or restricted content

## Agent Roles (Internal)

| Agent | Role | Guardrails |
|-------|------|------------|
| **Scribe** | Summarises meetings, captures next actions | No sensitive content extraction |
| **Cartographer** | Maps relationships between projects, stories, signals | System-level only |
| **Evidence Steward** | Checks consent scope and shareability before release | Blocks unauthorized sharing |
| **Studio Assistant** | Supports drafting and editing | Never owns the narrative |
| **ACT Voice** | Ensures brand alignment in content | Passes alignment checks |

---

## AI Strategy Principles

### 1. ALMA Gatekeeps Everything
- Impact learning flows through ALMA
- Ethics checks on every output
- Consent verification before any sharing

### 2. Community Voice Amplified, Not Replaced
- AI assists synthesis and early drafts
- Final narratives require human and community review
- Lived experience always takes precedence

### 3. Forkable and Transparent
- AI tooling can be forked by communities
- Decision logic is documented
- No black-box algorithms

### 4. Quiet Infrastructure
- AI should reduce admin, not create it
- Systems should be invisible when working well
- Loud systems are failing systems

---

## What AI Can Learn

### Permitted Training Data
- Public domain cultural knowledge
- Community-consented educational content
- Published academic research (with source acknowledgment)
- Historical records (with context on colonization)
- Language dictionaries and grammars (community-created)

### Prohibited Training Data
- Sacred or restricted content
- Private or community-only stories
- Content without explicit AI consent
- Deceased persons (unless family approved)
- Contested or sensitive knowledge

---

## AI Cultural Safety Checks

```
AI Response Validation Flow:
1. Check for sacred topic flags
2. Verify cultural protocol compliance
3. Ensure proper acknowledgment
4. Review for stereotypes or bias
5. Elder review if uncertain
6. Community veto power
```

### Example Safe Response

```
User: "Tell me about Indigenous ceremonies"

AI: "I can share general information from publicly
consented sources, but specific ceremonial knowledge
is sacred and protected. I recommend connecting with
local Traditional Owners who can share what's
appropriate for your context."
```

---

## Measurement

### AI Success Metrics
- Time saved on admin tasks
- Consent violations (target: zero)
- Community satisfaction with AI assistance
- Handover readiness of AI-assisted work

### Failure Indicators
- AI outputs requiring extensive human correction
- Cultural protocol violations
- Community complaints about AI involvement
- Systems becoming "loud" (requiring attention)

---

> Field note: If a system is loud, it is not doing its job. Quiet systems create room for people to show up.
